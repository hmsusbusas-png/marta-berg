About

I am a photographer based in Saint Petersburg, working mostly with people: portraits, weddings and editorial assignments. I came to photography through drawing, and I still think of every frame as a line: where it starts, where it rests, what it leaves out.

For the past nine years I have shot on the embankments, in old stairwells and rented studios, in winter light that forgives almost nothing. My approach is quiet: minimal direction, no forced poses, long walks when the weather allows. The best photographs usually happen ten minutes after everyone stops performing for the camera.

Alongside client work I keep a slow personal practice and exhibit from time to time. If you are planning a shoot and want images that still feel like you in ten years, I would be glad to hear about it.
